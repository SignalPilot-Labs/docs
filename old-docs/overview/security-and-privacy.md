Security and Privacy
Learn about the Security and the Privacy of SignalPilot

SignalPilot follows a zero data retention policy. Your code, data, and prompts are never stored on our servers. All AI assistance is powered by Claude models (Anthropic), and we do have zero data retention agreement with Anthropic. When using SignalPilot, your conversations with SignalPilot AI will not be used to train Anthropic’s models.

Our Commitments
🔒 Zero Data Retention: We do not save or retain your inputs or outputs.

🤝 Claude Only: All completions run on Anthropic’s Claude models.

🛑 No Server Storage: SignalPilot never stores code, queries, or database connections.

🪪 Privacy Stamp: Every session is covered by our Zero Retention Guarantee.

FAQ
Q: Does SignalPilot store my notebook code or data?
A: No. We have a strict zero retention policy—nothing is stored or logged.

Q: Which AI models does SignalPilot use?
A: Only Anthropic’s Claude family of models.

Q: Can I run SignalPilot offline?
A: No. The AI needs network access to connect to Anthropic’s servers.

Q: Is any data used for training?
A: No. Your data is never used to train models.
