# What is SignalPilot?

<Info>
This page establishes SignalPilot as a **Context Orchestration Engine** rather than just a Jupyter extension.
</Info>

## Context Orchestration for AI-native Data Teams

When leadership asks "why did remote assist interventions spike last week?" — the answer lives across your dbt models, S3 transformation logic, design docs, Jira tickets about recent changes, and Slack threads where your team already debugged a related issue.

### The Problem

Analysts spend 80% of investigation time re-collecting context that already exists somewhere. Claude, Cursor, and Copilot accelerate the last mile of writing code — but can't help with the first mile of knowing *what* to look for and *where* the truth lives.

### The Solution

SignalPilot is a Jupyter-native AI agent with a **context orchestration layer** purpose-built for data investigation. It can reason about your data because it has access to your full organizational context.

## How It Works at Your Company

<Steps>
<Step title="Analyst receives question">
  *"Why did on-road event detection rates change after last week's deploy?"*
</Step>

<Step title="SignalPilot agent investigates">
  - Identifies relevant dbt models and upstream S3 sources
  - Pulls the Jira ticket for the recent deploy and linked design doc
  - Finds a Slack thread where a teammate flagged a related anomaly
  - Checks transformation lineage for schema drift
</Step>

<Step title="Agent plans, iterates, and executes">
  Runs targeted queries to validate hypotheses — grounded in actual context, not hallucinated table names
</Step>

<Step title="Delivers actionable report">
  Investigation report your C-suite can act on — with provenance back to source systems
</Step>
</Steps>

## Why This Isn't Another Tool to Manage

Claude and Cursor are general-purpose coding assistants, while SignalPilot is purpose-built for data investigation — it runs where your analysis already lives (Jupyter, soon VS Code) and connects to context those tools can't access.

<Tip>
**Think of it as:** The difference between giving an intern a database connection vs. giving them 6 months of institutional knowledge about how your data actually works.
</Tip>
